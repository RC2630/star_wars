1. No institution shall have a monopoly on the Force. No institution shall monopolize either the Force as a whole, or one of the two (light/dark) sides of the Force. 
2. Force-sensitive individuals shall not be expected to give up their lives in service of the Force. Force-sensitive individuals are citizens of the galaxy, and as such should be given the same rights, freedoms, and responsibilies as any other citizen. One is encouraged to use their Force powers in aid of the galaxy, but this is neither are a requirement nor an expectation. 
3. The Dabi Order does not seek to impose any rules and restrictions on its members. Serious infringements and violations of common-sense ethics are generally illegal under Republic law already, and accordingly shall be handled through the Republic's judiciary system. 
4. The Dabi Order encourages natural behaviours and avoids imposing guidelines that violate "nature". For example, emotions are a natural part of every sentient being's life, and so shall not be repressed. Similarly for the desire for romance, urge for reproduction, etc. 
5. The Dabi Order emphasizes consent. Nobody shall be recruited into the Order against their wish. Nobody shall be prevented from leaving against their wish. Nobody shall be coerced into joining or leaving. Those incapable of making a sound decision (such as overly young individuals) are considered incapable of giving informed consent, and accordingly shall not be recruited into the Order or given training until they are old enough to understand the nature of their decision. 
6. The Force shall be treated as a gift rather than a burden. Those with a strong connection to the Force are said to be blessed, but they shall not be expected to do anything just because they are strong in the Force. 
7. The Dabi Order emphasizes individual autonomy over institutional uniformity. There shall be no council or other governing body overseeing the Order as a whole. Everyone is expected to make their own decisions. The Order does not believe that having a Grand Master or similar leader figure is necessary. 
8. The Dabi Order shall not, as a whole, take sides in galactic affairs or meddle in galactic politics or military forces. Individual members of the Dabi Order are permitted (although not necessarily encouraged) to participate in galactic events according to their views, conscience, and beliefs. 
9. The Dabi Order strongly believes in the value of a strongly bonded community. Members of the Dabi Order are encouraged to build lasting relationships among themselves, and to become close friends and help one another even outside the context of the Force and the Order's affairs. 
10. As the Force is larger than just the Dabi Order, the Order believes that dialogue and maintaining relationships with other Force institutions as well as individual Force users outside the Dabi Order throughout the galaxy are valuable. Exchange of knowledge, insights, experiences, and discussions about the Force are encouraged. The Dabi Order does not seek to view other Force institutions, particularly those similarly committed to the light side, as enemies or rivals.
