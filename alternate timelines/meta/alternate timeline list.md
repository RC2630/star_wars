# pre-episode I divergence

**`prequel era/pre-prequels divergence/dabi prequel.md`:**
in 40 BBY, 8 years before TPM, qui-gon is upset that the council would not permit his padawan obi-wan to marry satine kryze. this leads to qui-gon and obi-wan leaving the jedi order together, founding a rival light-side force institution called the Dabi Order which permits attachments and marriage. anakin is still found in 32 BBY (i.e. when he is 9 years old), but is folded into the Dabi instead of the Jedi.

**`prequel era/pre-prequels divergence/dabi maul branch.md`:**
a second Dabi timeline with more focus on maul. starts out the same as the Dabi timeline but diverges somewhere in the middle.

# episode I divergence

**`prequel era/episode I divergence/anakin serves padme branch.md`:**
on the flight out of tatooine, anakin refuses to become a jedi after qui-gon and obi-wan tell him the sacrifices that he would have to make as a member of the jedi order (including potentially never seeing his mother again). as a result, padme takes anakin into her own care, raising him inside the naboo palace and gradually training him as her personal assistant, eventually making him a member of her personal staff as she becomes a senator and heads to coruscant.

**`prequel era/episode I divergence/kill sidious after empire.md`:**
a second timeline with the same premise as the "anakin becomes padme's personal assistant and later joins her personal senatorial staff when she becomes senator" but dragged out a bit longer. the empire is proclaimed, and only after the empire do the protagonists get together to formulate a plan to get rid of him.

**`prequel era/episode I divergence/anakin as a naberrie.md`:**
the jedi council refuses to train anakin even after qui-gon's death. padme introduces anakin to her family. jobal and ruwee adopt anakin, and anakin grows up in that household.

**`prequel era/episode I divergence/anakin trained on mandalore.md`:**
the jedi council refuses to train anakin even after qui-gon's death. obi-wan refuses to break his promise to qui-gon, and chooses to leave the jedi order in order to train anakin himself outside the structure of the jedi order. obi-wan takes anakin to mandalore, where obi-wan and satine marry, and the two of them adopt anakin. then over many years, anakin receives force training and lightsaber training (and even a little bit of mandalorian combat training) from obi-wan.

**`prequel era/episode I divergence/anakin as king of naboo.md`:**
the jedi council refuses to train anakin after qui-gon's death, but obi-wan eventually accepts the council's ruling. anakin gets raised in naboo palace. when padme's term as queen is up, anakin runs for the election and gets elected as king of naboo at age 13. he eventually uses his position to free his mother from slavery on tatooine.

# episode II divergence

**`prequel era/episode II divergence/tatooine divergence/force healing.md`:**
anakin trained himself in the knowledge and ways of force healing ever since becoming a jedi. on tatooine, anakin uses his ability to save shmi when he reaches the tusken camp, allowing shmi to survive.

**`prequel era/episode II divergence/tatooine divergence/shmi survives (anakin doesn't fall).md`:**
anakin arrives at the tusken camp a bit earlier than in canon, where shmi is not so far beyond saving. he successfully retrieves her and takes her back to the lars homestead alive, where the lars family quickly take her into mos eisley for medical treatment in a bacta tank. ultimately shmi survived.

**`prequel era/episode II divergence/tatooine divergence/shmi survives (anakin falls).md`:**
a second Shmi survives because anakin arrives a bit earlier timeline, but this one has anakin actually fully fall to the dark side as in canon. the transition to OT plays similar to canon, except luke is raised by not just owen and beru, but also shmi (his actual grandmother) and cliegg. luke grows up hearing stories about anakin's childhood firsthand from shmi.

**`prequel era/episode II divergence/geonosis & wedding divergence/padme almost dies from gunship fall.md`:**
in AOTC, padme almost dies from her fall from the gunship (but anakin thought she actually died). this causes anakin to get mad at obi-wan for not stopping the ship and allowing him to rescue her. he leaves the jedi order in anger and spite, only to later find out that padme in fact survived the fall. he dedicates his life to padme.

**`prequel era/episode II divergence/geonosis & wedding divergence/bugged r2 exposes anakin's wedding.md`:**
obi-wan and yoda notice anakin and padme acting exceptionally close on geonosis, and they become suspicious. they put a bugging device in r2-d2, and using that, they covertly learned of anakin and padme's secret wedding. anakin is expelled from the jedi order, and padme resigns from the senate after the ensuing scandal. the two of them become independent operatives throughout the clone wars.

**`prequel era/episode II divergence/geonosis & wedding divergence/obi-wan interrupts anakin wedding.md`:**
obi-wan becomes increasingly suspicious of anakin and padme's relationship, and heads to naboo himself to investigate. he arrives at varykino villa just as anakin and padme are in the middle of their wedding, catching them red-handed. anakin leaves the jedi order after persuasion by obi-wan, and amid his sulking, padme offers him a place among her personal senatorial staff, which anakin accepts.

# clone wars divergence

**`prequel era/clone wars divergence/civilian oversight.md`:**
bail organa creates a system where senators are permanently paired with jedi generals to ensure civilian oversight over the military. bail is paired with obi-wan, and anakin is paired with padme.

**`prequel era/clone wars divergence/r2 falls into separatist hands.md`:**
early in the war, r2-d2 falls into separatist hands. dooku and grievous systematically examine the droid for military intelligence, but finds footage of anakin and padme's secret wedding, and chooses to broadcast that footage publicly to humiliate and discredit anakin and padme.

**`prequel era/clone wars divergence/anakin reads ROTS script.md`:**
during the clone wars, anakin receives a force vision in which he and padme travel to a strange planet and find a mysterious scroll. he takes padme and uses the force to locate the planet and scroll, which turns out to be a copy of the script of ROTS. eventually he authenticates it as genuine, and finds a way to edit it. thus anakin writes his own future, and writes all the tragedies (order 66, his own fall to the dark side, etc.) out of the future. as a result, those tragedies never end up happening.

**`prequel era/clone wars divergence/maul fails to capture obi-wan and satine.md`:**
maul attempted to capture obi-wan and satine, but fails. obi-wan and satine safely get away, and having experienced a near-death scenario, they pledge to prioritize each other and eventually marry. after the jedi council grant them an exception, anakin decides to try his own luck and reveals his own marriage, and receives an exception as well.

**`prequel era/clone wars divergence/anakin leaves jedi with ahsoka.md`:**
after ahsoka chooses to leave the jedi order following her trial because she becomes disillusioned with the jedi order, anakin leaves with her in solidarity.

# episode III (chancellor's office) divergence

**`prequel era/episode III divergence/chancellor's office divergence/anakin kills palpatine early.md`:**
anakin chooses to kill palpatine immediately on the spot the instant he learned he is a sith lord, rather than choosing to go inform mace and the other jedi masters.

**`prequel era/episode III divergence/chancellor's office divergence/mace kills sidious (no order 66).md`:**
mace kills palpatine in the chancellor's office duel.

**`prequel era/episode III divergence/chancellor's office divergence/mace kills sidious (order 66).md`:**
mace kills palpatine in the chancellor's office duel, but before he could complete his final strike, palpatine manages to get onto his comlink and broadcast "execute order 66" to all clone troopers.

**`prequel era/episode III divergence/chancellor's office divergence/order 60.md`:**
mace kills palpatine in the chancellor's office duel, but before he could complete his final strike, palpatine manages to get onto his comlink. he tries to say "execute order sixty-six" but mace lands his final strike before palpatine's final syllable can be uttered, rendering his command as "execute order sixty-..." and thus clones receive order 60 instead of order 66, which is a different contingency order. it is less harmful than order 66 but not totally harmless either - order 60 instantly discharges every clone trooper from the GAR and make them civilians.

**`prequel era/episode III divergence/chancellor's office divergence/mace senses anakin's betrayal.md`:**
just as anakin is about to cut off mace's hand, mace uses precognition to sense anakin's betrayal in time to stop him. mace kills palpatine then arrests anakin for attempting to attack a jedi council master.

**`prequel era/episode III divergence/chancellor's office divergence/anakin arrests mace.md`:**
mace kills palpatine in the chancellor's office duel. anakin arrives right after, gets mad at what mace did, and duels him. anakin wins, and then anakin arrests mace for killing the chancellor.

**`prequel era/episode III divergence/chancellor's office divergence/anakin arrives to find sidious won.md`:**
palpatine kills mace in the chancellor's office duel. anakin arrives right after, gets mad at what palpatine did, and duels him. anakin wins and kills palpatine himself.

# episode III (mustafar) divergence

**`prequel era/episode III divergence/mustafar divergence/padme discovers obi-wan on skiff.md`:**
padme discovers obi-wan on the skiff on the way to mustafar and confronts him for hiding on her skiff. in the confrontation, padme shoots obi-wan in the leg to prevent him from forcing her to take the ship to mustafar. as a result, anakin finishes slaughtering the separatists on mustafar then go back to coruscant. anakin says his end of the deal is complete and asks palpatine to teach him how to save padme. palpatine finally reveals that after all he doesn't actually know how to save padme. anakin gets angry at palpatine for misleading him, and they duel. anakin wins, and palpatine dies. anakin spends much of the rest of this timeline repenting for what he had done, especially what he did to the younglings in the jedi temple.

**`OT era/mustafar divergence/mustafar divergence timeline.md`:**
padme manages to talk down anakin on mustafar and convince him to abandon his dark path and stay with her. anakin and padme go into hiding and raise the twins themselves on naboo. anakin spends a lot of time repenting for what he had done. eventually naboo turns into a major base of operations for the rebel alliance as anakin and padme start rebelling against the empire, nearly causing naboo to be destroyed by the death star.

**`prequel era/episode III divergence/mustafar divergence/empress padme.md`:**
padme agrees to anakin's pitch of ruling the galaxy together. padme encourages anakin to betray and kill palpatine, following which she gradually takes over the office of emperor and becomes the galactic ruler. she then uses her position of power to gradually destroy the empire from within, slowly giving the senate more powers until the empire becomes an empire in name only. eventually the empire has become so democratic that she decided it's time to abolish imperial institutions altogether and reproclaim the republic.

**`prequel era/episode III divergence/mustafar divergence/episode III double duel reversal.md`:**
anakin kills obi-wan on mustafar, but yoda also kills sidious on coruscant at the same time. after the birth of the twins and padme's survival afterward, anakin let go of the dark side and pledges himself to padme. padme asks anakin to promise to follow her commands unconditionally. then padme starts playing 4D political chess, leveraging anakin's status as palpatine's sith apprentice to try to get him into the spot of legitimate successor of palpatine (and thus the new emperor), upon which she forces anakin to immediately abdicate in favour of her. then, as the galactic ruler, she does essentially the same thing as the last timeline, gradually dismantling the empire from within.

**`prequel era/episode III divergence/mustafar divergence/obi-wan rescues anakin on mustafar.md`:**
obi-wan defeats anakin on mustafar. but instead of leaving him to burn, he rescues the now limbless anakin and carries him back to padme's skiff. while padme undergoes delivery on polis massa, anakin is simultaneously getting new prosthetic limbs. both of them survive, the children are born, and anakin starts his redemption path. anakin and padme raise their children on naboo. a few years later, palpatine finds them and demands that anakin return to serving as his apprentice. anakin refuses, and a duel ensues, in which anakin defeats and kills palpatine.

# original trilogy divergence

**`OT era/early twin discovery/early twin discovery.md`:**
shortly after the battle of yavin, han solo leaves yavin 4 with his reward money to attempt to pay his debt to jabba. luke and leia start falling in love with each other, and eventually share a passionate kiss. r2-d2, who knows that luke and leia are siblings because his memory was never wiped, intervenes and shows the siblings an old recording of padme giving birth, which prove their siblinghood. upon learning this, luke and leia immediately end their fledgling relationship, and both of them commit to training as jedi together.

**`OT era/early twin discovery/ahch-to mission.md`:**
this is an extension of the above timeline, which takes place following the battle of endor. now that the empire is crumbling, luke and leia focus on rebuilding a jedi order together. they search many worlds throughout the galaxy for ancient jedi artifacts, archives, and knowledge. eventually, they learn about the first jedi temple on ahch-to, and go on an expedition to that planet.

**`OT era/early twin discovery/early full history discovery.md`:**
shortly after the battle of yavin, han solo leaves yavin 4 with his reward money to attempt to pay his debt to jabba. luke and leia start falling in love with each other, and eventually share a passionate kiss. r2-d2, who knows that luke and leia are siblings because his memory was never wiped, intervenes and shows the siblings an old recording of padme giving birth, which prove their siblinghood. upon learning this, luke and leia become curious about who their biological parents are. they look for answers but find no clues until pooja naberrie, leia's longterm friend and fellow imperial senator, puts the pieces together and concludes that leia's mother must be padme amidala, pooja's mother's sister. pooja takes luke and leia to naboo, where sola naberrie describes padme's background to the twins. after much clue finding and logical analysis, the twins realize that darth vader is most likely their father.

**`OT era/luke stays on dagobah.md`:**
luke senses his friends in pain while training with yoda on dagobah. he considers leaving to rescue them, but yoda and obi-wan's force spirit talks him down. luke remains on dagobah, continuing his training with yoda.

# sequel trilogy divergence

**`sequel era/luke kills ben sequels.md`:**
in 28 ABY, six years before canon TFA, luke senses darkness in the sleeping ben solo and considers killing him. unlike canon, he actually follows through with the kill, ending ben solo's life right there and then. the alternate timeline explores how the entire sequel trilogy would play out if ben died that early.

**`sequel era/rey and ben both survive.md`:**
in the final confrontation against palpatine and the final order, ben solo does not lose his life shortly after redeeming himself. both rey and ben emerge alive from the conflict, and together they rebuild a new jedi order and eventually marry each other.

**`sequel era/jawas find lightsabers.md`:**
at the end of canon TROS, rey buries luke and leia's lightsabers on tatooine. unfortunately, she didn't bury them deep enough, and a group of jawas soon find and scavenge the lightsabers. the lightsabers pass through the hands of jawas, collectors, hutts, bounty hunters, and eventually ends up in a tusken tribe.

**`sequel era/post-exegol capital and contest.md`:**
after the battle of exegol and the fall of the first order, the new republic is restored, but it has no capital due to the loss of hosnian prime to starkiller base. the core worlds of coruscant, chandrila, and corellia all submit a bid to become the new galactic capital. much later, the worlds of corellia, kuat, and mon cala enter a friendly shipbuilding contest to determine which planet makes the most impressive starships.

# alternate timelines for legends continuity

**`legends/teneniel survives poisoning/teneniel survives poisoning.md`:**
in official legends, in 27 ABY, teneniel djo is assassinated via poisoning by ta'a chume as part of a political intrigue, which leads to tenel ka djo becoming the new queen mother. this alternate timeline allows teneniel to survive the poisoning, and thus tenel ka is allowed to remain a full-time jedi and have time to pursue a relationship with jacen solo, rather than being forced to become the queen mother at age 18. the timeline explores how tenel ka's non-queen-mother status allows her to be closer to jacen, and ultimately prevent his fall to the dark side.

**`legends/teneniel survives poisoning/allana reads courtship of leia.md`:**
this timeline has the same premise as the above one, and by 39 ABY tenel ka and jacen's relationship have developed sufficiently for them to marry. allana djo solo, their daughter, receives a copy of "the courtship of princess leia" as a wedding gift from an anonymous donor. she falls in love with the book, as the book describes events that happened to her four grandparents.

**`legends/jaina jag hapes honeymoon.md`:**
in official legends, jaina solo and jagged fel marry in 44 ABY. jag planned a honeymoon on hapes, but jaina wanted to honeymoon on sakuub instead so she can go on a jedi expedition to retrieve a holocron at the same time; however, she promised that after the sakuub expedition the couple would spend the rest of their honeymoon on hapes. since this second leg of the honeymoon (the hapes portion) was never written in official legends, this timeline explores that leg of the trip that happened after sakuub.

**`legends/the chiss civil war.md`:**
in 46 ABY, raynur thul rejoins a killik nest, prompting worry from the chiss ascendancy due to his actions ten years prior during the swarm war. amid growing fear of the killiks, the chiss come into conflict with the jedi, and eventually an extremist chiss faction starts clashing with a more moderate chiss faction, sparking a full-scale chiss civil war.
