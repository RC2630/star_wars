# Part 1

The Battle of Coruscant raged across the skies of the Galactic Republic's capital. Turbolasers flashed through the darkness as Republic and Confederacy warships tore into one another in a struggle for control of the orbit. At the center of it all drifted the immense Separatist flagship *Invisible Hand*.

Deep within the vessel, in a heavily guarded chamber, Supreme Chancellor Palpatine sat bound to a chair exactly as he had intended. Count Dooku stood nearby with General Grievous, both awaiting the inevitable arrival of Jedi Knights Anakin Skywalker and Obi-Wan Kenobi.

Palpatine smiled.

"Good, good. Finally, all the pieces have come together. Soon the Sith shall rule the galaxy!"

Without warning, Dooku ignited his crimson lightsaber.

The blade flashed once.

Palpatine's eyes widened in disbelief as the lightsaber struck him cleanly. Before he could utter another word, his body slumped lifelessly against the restraints.

Silence filled the chamber.

General Grievous stared at the corpse, his mechanical eyes narrowing.

"What are you doing, Count Dooku?" the cyborg demanded. "Lord Sidious ordered us to not harm the Chancellor under all conditions."

Dooku calmly deactivated his weapon.

"I have decided to betray Lord Sidious."

Grievous tilted his head.

"But when Lord Sidious finds out that you killed the Chancellor, will he not punish us?"

Dooku looked directly at him.

"General Grievous... Lord Sidious is the Chancellor."

The general remained perfectly still.

"Am I hearing you correctly?"

"Yes."

For a brief moment Dooku glanced down at Palpatine's body before continuing.

"I don't have time to explain this right now, but just know this: under the Rule of Two that the Sith follows, the apprentice is expected to eventually betray their master so they can become the new master. I have decided that my time has come."

He folded his hands behind his back.

"For the Sith, what I did is only natural."

Grievous considered the explanation.

"Then why did you choose this moment?"

"Lord Sidious deliberately put himself in a vulnerable position by allowing us to abduct and restrain him here. If I strike now, he will be caught off guard and have no way to defend himself because of his restraints."

Dooku looked at the restraints that still held the dead Chancellor in place.

"So there is no better opportunity than now."

Grievous let out a low mechanical growl.

"Then why did Lord Sidious choose to do this?"

"Because he was too overconfident that I would not betray him."

Dooku's expression remained cold.

"Well, he was wrong. And he paid for his mistake with his life."

Neither of them spoke for several long moments.

Finally Grievous broke the silence.

"Shall we still wait for Skywalker and Kenobi to arrive?"

Dooku shook his head immediately.

"No. If they come and find out that we killed the Chancellor, they will not give us an easy time."

His voice grew more serious.

"General, these two Jedi are truly powerful. If we find ourselves in a duel with them... I am not confident that we will survive."

He turned toward the bridge.

"I advise that we flee instead."

Grievous nodded.

"Very well, my lord."

Moments later the *Invisible Hand* abruptly altered course. Republic crews watching the battle watched in surprise as the flagship broke away from the fighting instead of continuing toward Coruscant.

Anakin Skywalker and Obi-Wan Kenobi were still making their way through the battle when the massive vessel accelerated away from the engagement.

The *Invisible Hand* reached the edge of the combat zone and vanished into hyperspace.

Inside the blue tunnel of hyperspace, Dooku and Grievous stood together on the bridge.

Grievous folded his four mechanical arms behind his back.

"The Republic fleet has seen us flee. Sooner or later they will find out that we killed the Chancellor."

Dooku nodded.

"So we announce it ourselves on our own terms, rather than wait for them to find out."

He looked toward the stars streaking past outside.

"This way we can spin it into a propaganda victory for the Confederacy."

Grievous asked, "Shall we announce it now?"

"No."

Dooku remained patient.

"Let's get to Utapau first. Then we can make the announcement formally when we are ready."

"Very well, my lord."

The journey continued in silence.

Some time later, Dooku entered the chamber where Palpatine's body still lay.

He knelt beside the corpse and searched through the Chancellor's robes until he found a secure Republic comlink.

Holding it in one hand, Dooku said quietly,

"Let me try if the contingency order works."

He activated the device.

Encrypted frequencies appeared one after another, each corresponding to clone commanders and captains throughout the Republic military. The Chancellor had stored them all.

Dooku selected multiple channels simultaneously and began broadcasting.

"Execute Order 66."

For a moment nothing happened.

Then confused voices erupted across the communication network.

"You have no authority to issue that order to us!"

"This order can only be executed by the lawful Supreme Chancellor of the Republic!"

Another voice cut in.

"Count Dooku, is that you? You are our enemy! You cannot give us any orders!"

More protests followed from commanders across the galaxy, each refusing to acknowledge the command.

Dooku sighed and switched the comlink off.

"So Palpatine configured it so that only he can issue the order."

Grievous looked over curiously.

"What does it do?"

"It makes all of the Republic's clones turn against the Jedi and execute them."

Grievous gave a harsh metallic laugh.

"That sounds wonderful for us."

"But we can't use it."

Dooku slipped the comlink into his robes.

"Only Palpatine can use it."

Grievous shrugged.

"Never mind. Without Palpatine I fear that the Republic's forces shall collapse soon."

He spread two of his arms dramatically.

"With or without Order 66, I think the Republic is toast!"

Dooku allowed himself the faintest smile.

"I certainly hope so."

Some time later, the *Invisible Hand* emerged from hyperspace above Utapau and landed safely.

Once preparations had been completed, Count Dooku, General Grievous, and Viceroy Nute Gunray stood together before a holocamera. Their message was transmitted across the Confederacy of Independent Systems and beyond, reaching countless worlds throughout the galaxy.

Dooku stepped forward and began speaking.

"Dear citizens of the Confederacy and beyond, today we announce the death of the Supreme Chancellor of the Galactic Republic."

He paused briefly.

"As part of our legitimate military operation, we have captured the Chancellor and intended to take him back to the Confederacy to stand trial."

Grievous remained motionless beside him while Gunray folded his hands together.

"However," Dooku continued, "due to his relentless struggles and resistance despite his restraints, we had no choice but to use force on him, which due to his old age, unfortunately killed him by accident."

He maintained a solemn expression.

"While the Chancellor's death was not intentional, we believe that his years of aggression toward us and our cause justified his death."

He looked directly into the camera.

"We hope that all of our citizens and beyond can understand our situation."

Dooku concluded the address with calm confidence.

"Please carry on with your lives, and do not panic. We promise a swift victory over the Republic soon."

He raised his head proudly.

"Long live the Confederacy of Independent Systems!"

# Part 2

The *Invisible Hand* banked sharply away from the battle.

Anakin Skywalker's starfighter rolled after it immediately.

"Master!" he called over the comm. "They're changing course!"

Obi-Wan Kenobi's starfighter slid into formation beside him.

"Anakin, we can't let them get away!" Obi-Wan said urgently. "The Chancellor is still in there!"

"I know, Master, I'm trying!"

The Separatist flagship accelerated, pushing through the battle at full speed.

"Then try harder!"

Anakin gritted his teeth and shoved the throttle forward.

His engines screamed in protest.

"I'm already flying as fast as this thing can go."

R2-D2 let out an anxious string of electronic beeps from behind the cockpit.

"I know, Artoo. I'm doing everything I can."

The distance continued to widen.

Moments later, the *Invisible Hand* reached a safe jump point.

Blue light blossomed around the flagship.

Then it vanished into hyperspace.

Anakin stared at the empty stretch of space where it had disappeared.

Obi-Wan's voice came quietly over the comm.

"Well... I suppose we failed."

Anakin remained silent for a moment.

"What do we do now?"

Obi-Wan looked out across the battle still raging around Coruscant.

"There's still a battle to fight here. Even if we couldn't prevent them from taking the Chancellor away, at least we should stop them from taking Coruscant."

Anakin nodded.

"Right behind you, Master."

The two Jedi swung their starfighters back toward the battle.

For the next several hours they threw themselves into the defense of the Republic capital.

They intercepted waves of Vulture droids before they could reach civilian traffic.

They destroyed Hyena bombers attempting to cripple Republic cruisers.

Together they led squadrons of ARC-170s and V-wings through swarms of enemy fighters, breaking formation after formation.

At one point Obi-Wan calmly lined up a trio of Vulture droids and eliminated them with three precise bursts.

Anakin laughed over the comm.

"Still showing off, Master?"

"I was simply conserving ammunition."

"You fired three shots."

"Exactly."

Elsewhere Anakin flew straight through the debris of a destroyed frigate to pursue a fleeing tri-fighter.

"Anakin!" Obi-Wan shouted.

"I've got him!"

"You also have half a starship collapsing around you!"

The tri-fighter exploded under Anakin's fire just before he burst out the opposite side of the wreckage.

"I told you I had him."

Obi-Wan sighed.

"One day, your confidence will be the death of me."

As the battle wore on, the Separatist offensive gradually lost its coordination.

Without the *Invisible Hand* directing operations, individual commanders began making independent decisions.

One after another, Separatist warships disengaged.

Some retreated in organized formations.

Others fled chaotically, abandoning damaged vessels behind.

Eventually the last enemy ships jumped into hyperspace.

Silence slowly returned to the skies above Coruscant.

Republic rescue craft immediately spread throughout orbit, recovering escape pods and searching the wreckage.

Obi-Wan's voice returned over the comm.

"Let's go back to the Jedi Temple."

Anakin's reply came after a pause.

"What will they say when they find out?"

"Anakin, we can't stop everything ourselves. Sometimes certain things are simply beyond our control."

Obi-Wan spoke with quiet patience.

"You have to learn that you cannot get everything done by yourself."

"But Master Yoda..."

"I am sure Master Yoda will understand. Master Windu as well."

He smiled faintly.

"They have had their own failures throughout the Clone Wars. Nobody will punish us for letting the Separatists escape with the Chancellor."

Anakin looked down at the city-world stretching beneath him.

"I sure hope you are right."

Some time later, inside the Jedi Temple, the Council assembled in its familiar circular chamber.

Masters Yoda, Mace Windu, Plo Koon, Kit Fisto, Agen Kolar, and the other Council members listened as Obi-Wan and Anakin stood in the center of the room and recounted the battle from beginning to end.

When they had finished, silence settled over the chamber.

Yoda lowered his head.

"A bad day for the Republic, this is."

Mace Windu folded his arms.

"We don't know if the Chancellor is still okay. If we don't find out within a few hours, we need to send someone to infiltrate the Separatists to gather intel."

Kit Fisto nodded thoughtfully.

"The Separatists are likely going to press their attacks soon. We must be ready."

He looked toward the great windows overlooking Coruscant.

"We repelled them from Coruscant this time. But likely this is only because their true goal was abducting the Chancellor, and not actually conquering Coruscant."

His normally cheerful expression became serious.

"But next time... they may actually come to conquer our capital."

Agen Kolar added grimly,

"And take our Temple with them."

At that very moment the chamber doors slid open.

A Jedi Knight hurried inside, breathing heavily.

"Masters!"

Every member of the Council turned.

It was Berena.

She bowed quickly.

"We have just intercepted a broadcast from the Separatists in which they announced that the Chancellor has been killed!"

Shock spread across the room.

Yoda looked up.

"Show it to us, can you?"

Berena activated a holoprojector.

A blue holographic image of Count Dooku, General Grievous, and Nute Gunray appeared before the Council.

The chamber remained completely silent as the recorded message played from beginning to end.

No one interrupted.

No one spoke.

When the broadcast finally ended, Berena deactivated the projector.

The silence lingered.

Plo Koon spoke first.

"Well... looks like we need a new Chancellor."

He looked around the chamber.

"Once we get one, we can await orders from them to know what we should do next."

Mace Windu shook his head immediately.

"We have no time to wait for the election of a new Chancellor!"

His voice echoed across the chamber.

"If we don't act now, the Separatists will overrun us before the election even happens!"

Plo Koon remained composed.

"No. We cannot make executive decisions for the Republic."

He spoke calmly.

"We must wait for a Chancellor."

Mace frowned.

"And watch the Republic die?"

"The Republic is not going to die in a few days."

Plo met his colleague's gaze.

"Master Windu, please trust us."

Yoda slowly nodded.

"Agree with Master Koon, I do."

He rested both hands on his cane.

"Wait, we shall."

Mace remained silent for several long seconds.

Finally he exhaled.

"...Very well, Master Yoda."

As evening fell over Coruscant, Anakin left the Temple and made his way across the city to Padmé Amidala's apartment.

The door slid open.

"Hello, Padmé."

She looked up.

For an instant she simply stared.

Then she rushed toward him.

"ANAKIN!!"

She wrapped both arms around him.

"I have not seen you in five months!"

Anakin embraced her tightly.

"I missed you."

For several moments neither of them said anything.

Finally they stepped back.

Anakin's expression became more subdued.

"So... the Chancellor is dead."

Padmé nodded sadly.

"I heard."

"What do we do now?"

She walked toward the balcony overlooking the endless city lights.

"We elect a new Chancellor and keep going."

She looked out across Coruscant.

"The war is not over."

Her voice became quieter.

"And now it looks like it's not going to end anytime soon."

Anakin joined her.

"Are you going to run for the Chancellorship?"

Padmé smiled apologetically.

"No."

Anakin blinked.

"No?!"

She took one of his hands and gently placed it against her stomach.

"Ani..."

She smiled warmly.

"I'm pregnant."

For a heartbeat Anakin could only stare.

Then joy spread across his face.

"That's wonderful!"

Padmé laughed softly.

"The baby is due next month."

She squeezed his hand.

"I don't think I can devote myself to the Republic while caring for our baby at the same time."

She shook her head.

"Otherwise I most likely would have run."

Anakin nodded.

"That makes sense."

After a moment he asked,

"Who should we support then?"

"Senator Bail Organa."

She answered without hesitation.

"I sincerely hope that he can win this election."

She smiled.

"I trust him a lot, and I think he will be a great leader for the Republic."

"I agree."

Anakin nodded.

"I've worked with him a few times during the Clone Wars."

He smiled faintly.

"I respect him a lot."

Padmé hesitated.

"Ani... one other thing..."

"Yes, my angel?"

She looked into his eyes.

"After our child is born I think we should reveal our relationship to the public."

Anakin's smile faded.

"I really don't think we can hide each other and a child."

His expression grew troubled.

"But the Jedi Order will expel me!"

Padmé spoke gently.

"Sooner or later they will discover us or the child, and you will be expelled anyway."

She reached up and held his face in both hands.

"If we tell them on our own terms, maybe we can negotiate a... more graceful departure from the Jedi Order for you."

Anakin looked away toward the countless lights of Coruscant stretching to the horizon.

The silence between them lasted several seconds.

Finally he nodded slowly.

"I will think about it."

Padmé smiled with quiet relief.

"Thank you."

# Part 3

The death of Supreme Chancellor Palpatine had left the Republic shaken, but its institutions endured.

The very next day, campaigning for the office of Supreme Chancellor began.

For several days, senators from across the Republic debated the future before enormous crowds, addressed holonet audiences, and sought support from worlds weary after years of war.

Among all the candidates, Senator Bail Organa of Alderaan quickly emerged as the clear favorite.

Standing before packed assemblies throughout Coruscant, Bail spoke with calm determination.

"The Republic has suffered a terrible loss. We honor the memory of our former Chancellor by preserving the democracy that he served."

On another occasion he declared,

"We must bring this war to an end, but not at the cost of our principles. We will secure victory over the Separatists while protecting the freedoms that define the Republic."

His campaign centered on several clear promises.

He pledged to pursue an end to the Clone Wars.

He promised to ensure stability while safeguarding the Republic's democratic institutions.

He vowed to return the emergency powers that had accumulated in the office of the Chancellor back to the Senate.

He spoke repeatedly about strengthening the friendship and cooperation between the Senate and the Jedi Order.

Finally, he assured the public that the Republic would continue fighting until the Confederacy of Independent Systems had been defeated.

Many senators from every region of the Republic rallied behind him.

On election day, the result was overwhelming.

The Senate cast its votes.

Bail Organa won by a landslide.

As applause echoed throughout the Senate Rotunda, Bail stepped forward to take the oath of office.

"I accept this responsibility with humility."

He looked around the chamber.

"I will serve every citizen of the Republic to the best of my ability."

The Senate erupted into applause once again.

Thus Bail Organa became the new Supreme Chancellor of the Galactic Republic.

Several days later, another extraordinary session of the Galactic Senate convened.

This time, the attention of every senator focused upon the chamber entrance.

The great doors opened.

Ahsoka Tano entered first.

Beside her walked Captain Rex.

Between them, restrained by binders and surrounded by clone guards, walked Maul.

A murmur swept across the Senate floor.

Bail Organa rose from the Chancellor's podium.

"Ahsoka Tano. Captain Rex."

Both bowed respectfully.

Ahsoka spoke first.

"Chancellor, with the support of Captain Rex and the clone troopers of the 332nd Company, we successfully liberated Mandalore from Maul's control."

Rex continued.

"We captured him alive and transported him here to Coruscant to stand trial before the Republic."

Bail nodded.

"The Senate thanks both of you for your service."

He turned his attention toward the prisoner.

"Maul."

Maul stared back with open contempt.

"You have made quite the journey."

The former ruler of Mandalore merely smiled.

"I have."

Bail folded his hands.

"We have questions."

"So do I."

Ignoring the interruption, Bail continued.

"Is there anything you wish to tell this Senate before your trial begins?"

Maul laughed quietly.

"Oh, there certainly is."

He looked slowly around the enormous chamber.

"You should know that the Republic was controlled by a Sith Lord for over a decade!!"

The chamber exploded into whispers.

Bail raised a hand for silence.

"What do you mean?"

Maul's smile widened.

"Your Supreme Chancellor, Palpatine, was secretly the Sith Lord Darth Sidious, my original Sith master."

Many senators stared in disbelief.

"He has kept his Sith identity hidden from the Republic and the Jedi this entire time."

Bail studied Maul carefully.

"And why are you telling us this now?"

A shadow crossed Maul's face.

"Because I hate him!"

His voice echoed through the Senate.

"I hate Darth Sidious!"

He clenched his bound fists.

"When I was injured on Naboo, he threw me away like a piece of trash and replaced me with Count Dooku as his new apprentice."

Bitterness filled every word.

"I never forgave him for it."

A cruel smile returned.

"I am so happy that he was killed."

Bail remained composed.

"Do you want to collaborate with us against Count Dooku?"

He spoke evenly.

"We can offer you freedom under strict supervision if you help us defeat the Separatists."

Maul's answer came instantly.

"No! I will not."

"Why not?"

"Because I hate the Republic too!"

He glared toward the senators.

"And I hate the Jedi!"

His eyes settled on Ahsoka.

"I will never accept working with the Jedi."

Bail sighed quietly.

"Very well, then."

His expression hardened.

"Maul, you will be imprisoned while you await trial by the Supreme Court of the Republic."

He nodded toward the guards.

"Guards, please take him away!"

A squad of Senate Guards stepped forward.

They took hold of Maul's restraints.

The former Sith offered no resistance as he was escorted from the chamber.

Even after the doors closed behind him, the Senate remained filled with uneasy murmuring.

One senator stood.

"I don't believe him!"

Another immediately agreed.

"I don't believe him either!"

A third senator called across the chamber.

"Why should we trust him when he says Palpatine was a Sith Lord?"

"What evidence does he have?"

Several senators nodded.

Then another voice rose from elsewhere in the rotunda.

"I think we should at least investigate."

The chamber quieted.

"If there's even any sliver of chance that what he said is true... we need to know."

The debate continued for nearly an hour.

Finally Bail Organa stood.

"I believe we have reached a compromise."

The discussion ceased.

"A Senate committee will be formed to investigate the former Chancellor independently."

He looked around the chamber.

"The committee will determine whether there is any evidence supporting Maul's claims."

No serious objections followed.

The proposal passed.

Bail struck the podium gently.

"This session of the Senate is adjourned."

As senators gradually departed, Padmé Amidala made her way toward Ahsoka.

"Ahsoka."

Ahsoka turned.

"Senator Amidala."

"I need to ask a favor from you."

"Of course."

Padmé lowered her voice.

"You know that Anakin and I are married, right?"

Ahsoka nodded.

"Yes. Anakin told me."

Padmé smiled gently before placing a hand upon her stomach.

"Well, now I'm pregnant, and the baby is coming soon."

Ahsoka's face brightened.

"Congratulations."

"Thank you."

Padmé's smile faded into concern.

"I asked him to leave the Jedi Order so we can openly be together as a family."

Ahsoka tilted her head.

"Let me guess."

She smiled knowingly.

"He didn't agree?"

"He said he will think about it."

Padmé sighed.

"I think you should talk to him and try to convince him."

She looked directly at Ahsoka.

"He listens to you."

"He respects you."

"And most of all, you have already left the Jedi Order yourself."

Padmé gestured toward the Senate chamber they had just left.

"And after that, you have now successfully led Rex and the clones into battle, defeated Maul, liberated Mandalore, captured him, and brought him here."

She smiled warmly.

"You are the perfect person to show him that he can still be successful and do meaningful things even after leaving the Jedi Order."

Ahsoka considered her words before nodding.

"Okay."

She smiled.

"I will see what I can do."

Some time later, the two of them arrived at Padmé's apartment.

Anakin was already waiting.

The door opened.

He looked up.

"AHSOKA!!"

He hurried over.

"I heard you're back."

A broad grin spread across his face.

"How did Mandalore go?"

Ahsoka smiled.

"It went great."

She folded her arms.

"Maul was brutal."

She shook her head.

"That was perhaps one of the hardest duels I have ever fought."

Anakin's eyes shone with pride.

"But you defeated him."

"I did, Master."

He laughed.

"You don't have to call me that anymore."

Ahsoka smirked.

"Okay then, Skyguy."

Padmé chuckled.

"I've missed hearing that."

The three settled into the common room.

Anakin leaned forward eagerly.

"So how did he fight?"

Ahsoka began demonstrating with her hands.

"He constantly tried to control distance."

She stood.

"He wanted me to overcommit."

Anakin nodded.

"Classic Maul."

"I realized that if I kept chasing him, he'd dictate the fight."

"So you made him come to you?"

"Exactly."

They spent the next several minutes discussing footwork, timing, feints, defensive positioning, and the rhythm of lightsaber combat.

Eventually the conversation drifted elsewhere.

Anakin leaned back.

"How has life been for you after leaving the Jedi Order?"

Ahsoka thought for a moment.

"It was surprisingly great."

She smiled softly.

"I did miss some of my Jedi friends from time to time."

She looked around the apartment.

"But now I have a lot more freedom than before."

She shrugged.

"It feels good that there aren't always orders from above that I have to follow anymore."

Silence settled over the room.

Then Ahsoka spoke again.

"I think you should leave the Jedi Order too."

Anakin slowly turned toward Padmé.

"You told her to say this, didn't you?"

Padmé answered honestly.

"I did."

She reached over and took his hand.

"But I think it's for your own good."

She looked at Ahsoka.

"And Ahsoka agrees with me."

"Right, Ahsoka?"

"Yes."

Anakin looked from Padmé to Ahsoka.

Neither of them looked away.

After a long silence, Ahsoka continued.

"I left the Jedi Order."

She counted on her fingers.

"And I was still able to work with Rex."

"And I was still able to command the clone troopers of the 332nd Company."

"And I was still able to go on a campaign to Mandalore."

"And I was still able to free Mandalore from Maul, duel him, capture him, bring him to justice, and achieve victory."

She smiled.

"Skyguy, you don't have to be in the Jedi Order to do any of those things."

Anakin hesitated.

"But Obi-Wan..."

Ahsoka answered immediately.

"Obi-Wan would certainly agree with me and Padmé if he knows about the full situation."

She smiled reassuringly.

"And leaving the Jedi Order doesn't mean you can never talk to Obi-Wan again."

Anakin lowered his eyes.

For a long time he said nothing.

Finally he took a slow breath.

"Fine."

He looked at both of them.

"I will leave the Jedi Order."

Relief immediately spread across Padmé's face.

Ahsoka smiled broadly.

Padmé squeezed Anakin's hand.

"Tomorrow we go to the Temple and tell them."

Anakin nodded.

"So be it."

Ahsoka nodded as well.

A little later, after the conversation had relaxed once again, Padmé turned toward Ahsoka.

"Ahsoka... one more thing..."

"Yes?"

"Do you have a place to live right now?"

Ahsoka hesitated.

"...No, not really."

She smiled awkwardly.

"I have lived in the Jedi Temple for almost my whole life."

She shrugged.

"After I left the Order, I have been mostly just drifting around."

Padmé smiled warmly.

"I invite you to stay with us here in this apartment while you look for a new permanent home for yourself."

Anakin immediately nodded.

"Snips, I would love that."

He smiled.

"Would you please stay with us?"

Ahsoka looked around the apartment.

For the first time in many months, the idea of having somewhere to belong appealed to her more than she expected.

After a thoughtful pause, she smiled.

"Sure."

"I would be happy to."

That evening, Padmé prepared the couch in the common area with fresh blankets and pillows for Ahsoka.

The apartment, which had often felt quiet and lonely during the long years of war, was filled once again with familiar conversation, shared laughter, and stories from distant battlefields.

For the first time ever, Anakin, Padmé, and Ahsoka spent a peaceful and quiet night together under the same roof.

# Part 4

The following morning, Anakin Skywalker, Padmé Amidala, and Ahsoka Tano crossed the broad entrance hall of the Jedi Temple together.

Many Jedi recognized Anakin and greeted him as he passed. Others noticed Padmé and Ahsoka accompanying him and exchanged curious glances, but no one questioned the three of them as they made their way toward the Council chamber.

A Temple attendant bowed.

"The Council is ready to receive you."

The great doors slid open.

Masters Yoda, Mace Windu, Obi-Wan Kenobi, Plo Koon, Kit Fisto, Agen Kolar, and the remaining members of the Jedi Council looked toward the visitors.

Yoda spoke first.

"Welcome. Requested an audience, you have."

Anakin nodded.

"Yes, Master."

He stepped into the center of the chamber, with Padmé and Ahsoka standing quietly behind him.

"There are some things I need to tell all of you."

The Council waited patiently.

Anakin took a deep breath.

"Padmé Amidala is my wife."

Several members of the Council exchanged surprised looks.

"We have been secretly married for three years already."

Silence filled the chamber.

Anakin continued before anyone could respond.

"There's more."

He reached over and gently took Padmé's hand.

"Padmé is pregnant."

This revelation brought another round of stunned silence.

Finally Anakin spoke the words he had rehearsed all morning.

"For those reasons... I have decided to resign from the Jedi Order."

No one answered immediately.

Obi-Wan lowered his eyes.

His expression was filled not with anger, but sadness.

He had suspected the truth for years.

Now that Anakin had spoken it openly before the Council, there was no path back.

The rules of the Order were clear.

Even if Anakin wished to remain, he no longer could.

After several long moments, Obi-Wan quietly said,

"I understand."

His voice carried obvious sorrow.

"But now that you have revealed your marriage before the Council... you can no longer remain a member of the Order."

He managed a small, melancholy smile.

"I wish things had been different."

Anakin looked at his former master.

"So do I."

Yoda rested both hands upon his cane.

"Your choice, this is."

Mace Windu nodded solemnly.

"You have served the Jedi faithfully for many years."

One after another, both leaders signed the document.

The treaty formally recognized the sovereignty of both the Galactic Republic and the Confederacy of Independent Systems.

It established that every territory controlled by either government at the moment the ceasefire had taken effect would henceforth be recognized as the lawful territory of that state.

When the final signatures had been completed, both leaders rose.

After a brief pause, Bail extended his hand.

Gunray looked at it for only a moment before accepting it.

The two shook hands.

Applause gradually filled the chamber.

Nearly six years after the outbreak of the Clone Wars, the conflict had finally come to an end.

The galaxy entered a new era.

Two great powers now stood side by side.

The Galactic Republic.

The Confederacy of Independent Systems.

Neither trusted the other completely.

Both maintained powerful militaries.

The peace between them remained fragile.

Yet after so many years of devastating conflict, almost no one wished to see another war begin.

For the first time in years, there was genuine hope that the peace might endure.

Life slowly settled into a new rhythm.

On Coruscant, Luke and Leia Skywalker grew from infants into energetic toddlers.

Their laughter filled the apartment that had once served as a quiet refuge during the war.

R2-D2 faithfully followed them from room to room, chirping happily whenever they chased after him.

C-3PO devoted himself to teaching them words, manners, and stories, though he often found himself interrupted by curious little hands pulling at his arms.

"Oh dear," Threepio would say with theatrical exasperation. "Master Luke, please do be careful with my elbow."

Luke giggled.

Leia smiled mischievously before hiding behind R2.

The astromech answered with a cheerful whistle.

Padmé continued serving in the Galactic Senate.

Although much of her time remained devoted to raising Luke and Leia, she also continued working toward the Republic's future.

One evening she looked over a stack of legislative proposals while Anakin carried Leia through the living room.

He smiled.

"Working again?"

Padmé returned the smile.

"Always."

She looked toward their children.

"When they're a little older..."

She closed the datapad.

"I think I'll run for Chancellor."

Anakin nodded.

"I think you'd be great."

After the war, Ahsoka Tano found a home of her own on Coruscant.

Though she no longer lived with Anakin and Padmé, she remained a frequent visitor.

To Luke and Leia, she quickly became a familiar and beloved presence.

Whenever she visited, the twins eagerly ran to greet her.

"Ahsoka!"

She laughed and knelt to embrace them.

"I missed you two."

Freed from the obligations of serving any formal institution, Anakin and Ahsoka often traveled together throughout the galaxy with R2-D2.

When disputes threatened to become violent, they offered mediation.

When pirates preyed upon shipping lanes, they intervened.

When worlds requested experienced lightsaber wielders to help resolve dangerous situations, they answered the call.

They took orders from no government, no military, and no Order.

Instead, they chose their own missions wherever they believed they could do the most good.

Many worlds came to know them simply as two experienced heroes and a loyal astromech who appeared when they were needed and departed once the crisis had passed.

Whenever those journeys ended, Anakin and R2-D2 always returned home.

There, Padmé, Luke, Leia, and C-3PO were waiting.

The war that had defined so much of their lives had ended.

At last, they could simply be a family.
